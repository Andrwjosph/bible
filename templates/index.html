Help & Tips

Getting Started

Welcome to Bible AI Chat! Here are some tips to help you get the most out of your experience:

  • Click on any suggestion card to start a conversation
  • Type your question in the input box and press Enter or click Send
  • Use the "Explain Further" button to get more detailed answers
  • Rate answers using the thumbs up/down buttons to help improve responses

Features

  • Chat History: Access your previous conversations from the sidebar
  • New Chat: Start a fresh conversation by clicking the "New Chat" button
  • Copy Answers: Click the copy icon to copy any answer to your clipboard
  • Settings: Customize your experience in the Settings menu
